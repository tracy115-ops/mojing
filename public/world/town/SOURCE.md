# Bundled town props

Source: [Kenney Fantasy Town Kit 2.0](https://kenney.nl/assets/fantasy-town-kit), created/distributed by Kenney, 2025-08-03.

License: CC0 1.0. The original notice is retained in `LICENSE.txt`.

MoJing assembles walls, roofs and props into reusable GLB prefabs, merges their static geometry and embeds the palette texture. These assets require no external download when the application runs. They are low-poly models, not scanned or photorealistic environments.

To reproduce: unpack the original pack, then run `node scripts/build-world-town-assets.mjs <pack-directory>`.
